At Philanthropy Leaders, that means owning the day-to-day IT of a small team, from user accounts and access rights to email authentication and password management, and writing down every decision so the next person doesn't start from zero.

Ma carrière repose sur une habitude : comprendre comment les choses fonctionnent vraiment avant d'y toucher.
